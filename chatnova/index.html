FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How fast can we set it up?

Most teams are live in under 10 minutes. Connect your knowledge sources, paste one line of code, and you're done.

Will the AI get my brand voice right?

Yes. ChatNova learns tone from your existing content and tickets. You can also set explicit style rules — friendly, formal, technical, etc.

What happens when AI can't answer?

It hands off to your team with full context — the conversation, the customer profile, and a suggested answer for your teammate.

Is our data used to train public models?

Never. Your data trains only your assistant, is encrypted at rest, and is never shared. Enterprise plans can add private model deployment.

Which platforms does the widget support?

Any website, React/Vue/Angular app, Shopify store, or native mobile app. There's also an API for full custom control.
