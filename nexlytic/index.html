FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to set up?

Most customers connect their first data source in under 5 minutes. You'll typically see your first AI insight within 15 minutes of signing up. Full integrations with all your tools usually take under an hour.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. If you can connect a Google account, you can use Nexlytic. All integrations are one-click, and every insight is explained in plain English with the reasoning behind it.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. You can cancel from your dashboard in two clicks — no phone calls, no retention flow, no hard feelings. Your data is exportable at any time.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Nexlytic is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We never sell your data, and you can request deletion anytime.

What happens after my free trial?

Before your trial ends we'll email you a summary of what you've built. Choose a plan that fits, or your workspace automatically pauses — your data stays safe for 90 days.
