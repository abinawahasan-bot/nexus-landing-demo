FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a technical background?

Not at all. If you can post on Instagram, you can run a Shopari store. Everything is drag-and-drop.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes — on Grow and Enterprise plans you connect your domain with free SSL in about 2 minutes.

What payment methods can my customers use?

Cards, Google Pay, Apple Pay, bank transfers, e-wallets, and 20+ local methods across 135+ currencies.

How long does it take to launch?

Most brands launch within a day. Add products, pick a theme, connect payments — you're live.

Can I migrate from another platform?

Yes. Our free importer moves products, customers, and orders from Shopify, WooCommerce, and CSV files.
