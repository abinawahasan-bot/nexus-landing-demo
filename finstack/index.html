FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is FinStack PCI compliant?

Yes. FinStack is a PCI DSS Level 1 certified service provider. Card data never touches your servers — we handle it with tokenization.

How fast are payouts?

Standard payouts settle in 1 business day; instant payouts settle in under 2 minutes in 120+ countries, 24/7.

Which payment methods do you support?

Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay), bank transfers, and 20+ local methods across 135+ currencies.

Can I migrate from another processor?

Absolutely. Our migration guide and API compatibility layer make most switches a weekend project. Most customers report zero downtime.

What does "no monthly fee" mean?

You only pay a small percentage per transaction. No subscription, no setup fee, no minimums. Enterprise custom pricing available.
