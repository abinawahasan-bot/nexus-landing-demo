Frequently asked questions

No. Every path starts at beginner level and adapts to your pace. You can also test out of topics you already know.

Can I learn on my phone?

Yes — iOS, Android, and any browser. Lessons sync instantly, and Pro includes offline downloads for commutes.

How do certificates work?

Pass the final assessment and you earn a unique, verifiable certificate — easy to add to LinkedIn or a CV.

Is the free plan really free?

Really free — no credit card required. You get 5 courses and full quiz access, forever.

Yes — the Teams plan supports bulk seats, admin analytics, and SSO. Many companies and schools use it.
